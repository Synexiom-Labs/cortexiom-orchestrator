### Performance Bonus Program

Performance bonuses are paid annually based on:
- Net new assets brought in during the calendar year
- Client retention rate
- Compliance incident record (clean record required to be eligible)
- Regional ranking

**Bonus range:** $8,000–$47,000 CAD per year depending on performance tier.
**Average advisor bonus (2025):** $22,400 CAD

**Current disclosure method:**
Performance bonuses are disclosed in **Appendix C** of the client Relationship Disclosure Document (RDD), under the heading *"Additional Compensation"*:

> *"Your advisor may receive additional incentive compensation based on business performance metrics. This compensation is subject to regulatory requirements and is governed by our internal compensation policy."*

**Legal opinion on Appendix C disclosure:**
Internal legal memo (March 2023): *"The Appendix C disclosure meets the regulatory requirements under NI 31-103 s.13.4 as it alerts clients to the existence of performance-based compensation. No specific dollar amounts are required. Disclosure is adequate."*

## Compliance Flag — August 2024 CSA Guidance

**Issue identified:** CSA Staff Notice 31-362 (August 2024) tightened the disclosure standard for performance bonuses. The new standard requires:
- Plain language (not regulatory boilerplate)
- Specific dollar range or formula
- Explanation of how the bonus could affect the advice given
- "Reasonable client" test — would a retail client understand the conflict from reading this?

**Assessment:** The current Appendix C language ("may receive additional incentive compensation based on business performance metrics") does not:
- State the dollar range ($8,000–$47,000)
- Explain in plain terms how a bonus creates a potential conflict (e.g., "Your advisor may have a financial incentive to recommend products that increase your invested assets, as this increases their performance bonus.")
- Meet the reasonable client test for a retail investor

**Reliance on March 2023 legal opinion:** The 2023 legal opinion was rendered under the pre-2024 standard. It does not address the CSA Staff Notice 31-362 requirements, which were issued 17 months after the opinion.

**Current status:** No update to Appendix C or client disclosure has been made following the August 2024 CSA guidance. The compliance team flagged this as a Q3 2026 action item in the annual compliance plan.

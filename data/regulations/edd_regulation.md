# Enhanced Due Diligence — Politically Exposed Persons
**Regulatory Reference:** FINTRAC Compliance Guidance — AMLTF-2024-03
**Supersedes:** AMLTF-2019-11
**Effective:** July 1, 2024

---

## 1. Overview

Financial entities subject to FINTRAC reporting obligations must apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) to all Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their immediate family members. This guidance clarifies the four-component EDD framework and documentation requirements introduced under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act amendments (Bill C-83).

## 2. Definitions

**Politically Exposed Person (PEP):** Any individual who holds or has held, within the preceding five years, a prominent public position including head of state, senior government official, judge of a superior court, senior military officer, or director/executive of a state-owned enterprise.

**Domestic PEP (DPEP):** Canadian PEP
**Foreign PEP (FPEP):** Non-Canadian PEP — subject to higher scrutiny requirements
**Head of International Organization (HIO):** Leader of a recognized international body

## 3. Four-Component EDD Framework

Compliance with EDD obligations requires **all four components** to be completed and documented separately. Completion of one component does not satisfy requirements for others.

### Component 1 — Source of Funds (SOF)
Documentation that identifies the origin of assets being deposited or invested:
- Bank statements (minimum 3 months, 12 months for accounts > $500K)
- Employment records, salary confirmation, or business income documentation
- For self-employed: corporate financial statements, tax returns
- **Required for**: All PEPs regardless of account value
- **Documentation standard**: Original or certified copy; third-party verification required for accounts > $1M

### Component 2 — Source of Wealth (SOW)
Documentation of how the client accumulated their overall net worth:
- Asset history and acquisition timeline
- Inheritance documentation where applicable
- Business ownership and sale records
- **Required for**: Foreign PEPs and accounts > $500K; recommended for all PEPs
- **Documentation standard**: Narrative declaration + supporting documentation

### Component 3 — Adverse Media Screening (AMS)
Systematic screening against:
- Global adverse media databases
- Regulatory sanction lists (OFAC, OSFI, UK HM Treasury)
- Enforcement actions and court records
- **Coverage**: Minimum 5-year lookback; global coverage required for FPEPs
- **Frequency**: At onboarding + annual refresh + triggered by material transaction
- **Documentation standard**: Screening report with database names, dates, and results (positive or clear)

### Component 4 — Enhanced Monitoring (EM)
Ongoing transaction monitoring at elevated thresholds:
- Quarterly transaction review by a designated compliance officer
- Annual re-evaluation of risk rating
- Immediate review triggered by: threshold transactions, unusual patterns, adverse media alerts
- **Documentation standard**: Monitoring log with reviewer name, date, and summary findings

## 4. Documentation Requirements

Each component must be documented in the client file with:
1. **Component name** (SOF / SOW / AMS / EM)
2. **Completion date**
3. **Reviewer name and role**
4. **Outcome** (Satisfactory / Requires Follow-Up / Escalated)
5. **Supporting documents** referenced or attached

A single "EDD completed on [date]" entry **does not meet** documentation requirements. The absence of component-level records constitutes a documentation deficiency and creates regulatory exposure regardless of whether the underlying due diligence was performed.

## 5. Annual Refresh

All EDD components must be refreshed annually or upon:
- Significant change in the PEP's public role
- Material transaction above reporting thresholds
- Adverse media alert
- Client-initiated change in account structure

## 6. Failure to Comply

Incomplete EDD documentation may result in:
- FINTRAC administrative monetary penalty (AMP) up to $500,000 CAD per violation
- Referral to law enforcement where money laundering is suspected
- Suspension of the client relationship pending remediation
- Public naming in FINTRAC enforcement proceedings

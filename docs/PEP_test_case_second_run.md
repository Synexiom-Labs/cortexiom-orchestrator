- **Operational/Infrastructural Gap:** The complete failure of file retrieval means it is impossible to differentiate between EDD measures that were "conducted but misfiled" and those that were "never substantively conducted." Both scenarios carry severe regulatory implications.
- **Epistemic Gap:** It is currently unknown whether the institution's EDD program is calibrated to FINTRAC's evidentiary interpretation of 'reasonable measures' or merely to internal checklist standards. Recovered files, even if found, may still be non-compliant if they don't meet FINTRAC's specific substantiation requirements, particularly for Foreign PEPs.
- **Irreducible Risk – Retroactivity:** FINTRAC may interpret the absence of contemporaneous EDD documentation as affirmative evidence that the required measures were not taken at the time. Retrospective reconstruction, however thorough, may not fully mitigate this compliance exposure. This elevates the issue beyond simple remediation to one requiring senior leadership and legal involvement for risk assessment, quantification, and potential disclosure strategies.

Given the severity and systemic nature of the findings, a structured and prioritized approach is critical.

    - **Action:** Immediately provide access to the PEP client register and any associated procedural documentation that explains the meaning and attestation process behind the 'completion dates' entries.

    - **Action:** Brief senior management and legal counsel on the current 'unverifiable' compliance status due to inaccessible EDD documentation, the critical operational gap in record-keeping, and the potential for significant FINTRAC penalties should non-compliance be confirmed. Emphasize the "Irreducible Risk – Retroactivity" highlighted by Cortexiom.
    - **Action:** Develop a strategic plan for how to address this systemic issue, including potential proactive engagement with FINTRAC if advised by legal counsel.
- **P2: FINTRAC EDD Standard Calibration (Owner: Compliance Department; Timeline: 1 Week Post-Briefing)**
    - **Action:** Conduct a formal review and calibration of the institution's internal EDD standards against FINTRAC's most current interpretive guidance and enforcement expectations for all four EDD components. This addresses the "Epistemic Gap."
    - **Action:** Document FINTRAC's specific evidentiary expectations for each component (SOF, SOW, AMS, EM) to serve as the benchmark for all future and retrospective reviews.
- **P2: Comprehensive EDD File Review (Owner: Compliance Team, External Reviewers (if necessary); Timeline: 2-4 Weeks Post-Document Retrieval)**
    - **Action:** Once *all available* documentation is retrieved, conduct a thorough, component-by-component review of each PEP client file against the *calibrated FINTRAC EDD standards*. Prioritize identification of Foreign PEPs (FPEPs) for enhanced scrutiny.
    - **Action:** Document specific deficiencies for each of the four components (SOF, SOW, AMS, EM) per client, including missing elements, inadequate evidence, or outdated information.
- **P3: Remediation Plan Development & Execution (Owner: Compliance Team, Business Units; Timeline: 1 Week Post-Review, Ongoing)**
    - **Action:** Develop client-specific remediation plans to address identified documentation gaps. This may involve:
        - Re-collecting missing SOF/SOW documentation from clients.
        - Conducting new or updated AMS.
        - Implementing enhanced monitoring procedures for high-risk clients.
        - Obtaining missing senior management approvals (if permissible retroactively).
    - **Action:** Implement and track the execution of all remediation plans diligently.
- **P3: Policy, Procedure & Training Overhaul (Owner: Compliance Department; Timeline: Ongoing)**
    - **Action:** Review and update all internal EDD policies, procedures, and record-keeping protocols to ensure full alignment with FINTRAC requirements and to prevent recurrence of this systemic failure.
    - **Action:** Implement mandatory, comprehensive training for all relevant staff on the updated EDD policies, documentation standards, and record-keeping requirements.
